Cafeine

Keeps your Mac awake from the menu bar: one click on the cup, and the Mac stops going to sleep until you click again or the time you chose runs out.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT) · macOS 13 or later · signed and notarized · English and French

Why Cafeine

One click

Click the cup in the menu bar to keep the Mac awake; click again to let it sleep. A filled cup means it is on.

For as long as you need

Indefinitely, or for 15 minutes up to 8 hours. When the time runs out, the Mac sleeps again on its own.

Screen off, Mac on

Allow Display Sleep keeps the system awake while the screen turns off — for a download or a long job.

About 12 MB

No child process, no notifications, no update checker, no settings window. One power assertion, released if the app ever quits.

Pick a duration

Right-click the cup for the menu: no limit, or 15 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours. Open at Login is there too. From a script, open -a Cafeine --args -activate 3600 starts it already on.

Nothing else

No Dock icon, no window, no notifications piling up. The About panel is the only window Cafeine has. English, French, German, Spanish and Italian, following the system language.

Get it

Download the .dmg Read the README En français

Open the .dmg, drag Cafeine onto Applications. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple: it opens with a double click.
